# Project Defination
```
A unique, temporary effort that has a definite begining and a definite ending with several related and/or interdependent tasks to create a product or service that adds business value and helps customers accomplish their desired goals
```
## keywords
- effort
- definite begining
- definite ending
- several tasks
- create product or servie
- add business value
- help customers accomplish goals

## Triple constraint
- Quality
    - Budget/Cost
    - Scope
    - Time/Schedule

## Project definition
- Has a unique purpose
- Is temporary
- Divers change and enables value creation
- Is developed using progressive elaboration
- Requires resources, often from various areas
- Should have a primary customer or sponsor
- Involves uncertainty

## stakeholders
- people or groups who can impact or be impacted by the work or results of project
