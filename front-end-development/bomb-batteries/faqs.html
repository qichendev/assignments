How do I know when my battery needs to be replaced?

Common signs that your battery may need replacement include:

  • Slow engine crank when starting your vehicle
  • Dim headlights or interior lights
  • Battery/Check Engine warning light on your dashboard
  • Swollen or bloated battery case
  • Battery that is more than 3-5 years old
  • Devices running out of power much faster than normal

If you're experiencing any of these symptoms, we recommend bringing your battery in for a free test at any of our locations.

What's the difference between standard and deep cycle batteries?

Standard (starting) batteries are designed to deliver short, high-current bursts of power to start engines, and then be quickly recharged by the alternator. They have thinner, more numerous lead plates to maximize surface area.

Deep cycle batteries are designed to provide steady power over a long period and can be deeply discharged and recharged many times. They have thicker lead plates that withstand repeated discharge cycles. Deep cycle batteries are ideal for applications like marine trolling motors, RVs, golf carts, and solar power systems.

How long do batteries typically last?

Battery lifespan varies by type and usage:

  • Automotive batteries: 3-5 years
  • Marine/RV batteries: 2-6 years
  • Alkaline (AA, AAA, etc.): 5-10 years shelf life
  • Rechargeable NiMH: 500-1000 charge cycles
  • Lithium-ion: 300-500 charge cycles or 2-3 years
  • Deep cycle lead-acid: 200-500 cycles

Factors affecting battery life include temperature extremes, charging habits, usage patterns, and storage conditions.
