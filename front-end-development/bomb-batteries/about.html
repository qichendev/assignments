About Bomb Batteries

Founded in 2005, Bomb Batteries has grown from a small local shop to one of the leading battery suppliers in North America, serving both retail and commercial customers with the highest quality power solutions.

Our Story

Bomb Batteries began when founder Jack Zhang saw a need for reliable, affordable batteries in an increasingly power-dependent world. Starting with just a small storefront and a commitment to quality, we've grown to serve over 50,000 customers annually across the continent.

Why Shop With Us

  1. Extensive selection of batteries for every application
  2. Industry-leading warranties
  3. Expert technical advice
  4. Competitive pricing
  5. Fast shipping options
  6. Environmental commitment with our recycling program

Our Guarantee

We stand behind every product we sell with our 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your purchase for any reason, we'll make it right. That's the Bomb Batteries promise!
