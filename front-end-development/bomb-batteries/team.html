Our Story

Bomb Batteries was founded in 2005 by Jack Zhang, who recognized the growing demand for reliable power solutions in an increasingly electronic world. What started as a small shop with just three employees has grown into a nationwide operation with over 50 team members.

Our journey has been one of continuous innovation and growth, always focused on our core mission: providing the highest quality batteries at competitive prices with exceptional customer service.
