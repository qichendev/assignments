## Project Report Structure and Content

Your project report should include the following four sections. You are welcome to add additional sections if needed.

### 1. Abstract/Summary

The abstract is the most important component of the report. It summarizes the entire report in one or two paragraphs.

### 2. Statement of Need

This part can be borrowed from your proposal.

- What is the issue that you are addressing, and why does it matter?
- Why is what you propose necessary?
- Who benefits? Indicate the public good, not just the effect on campus.

### 3. Project Technical Report

This section is among the most important sections and includes the majority of your content. Here you will need to explain, step by step, the entire project and the actions you have taken to complete the project.

Include details such as:

- What services are used
- What technology stack is used
- What programming languages are used
- What libraries, SDKs, or APIs are used

You will also need to accompany your content with appropriate code snippets, screenshots, graphics, diagrams, or any other content that adds value to your textual content.

### 4. Project Result

In this section, demonstrate the final results of the project. Screenshots, figures, and explanations are the best options for conveying the result of your project to stakeholders.
