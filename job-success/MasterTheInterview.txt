• The STAR method is a great way to structure your
answers to behavioral questions.
• Describe the Situation you were in, the Task you needed
to accomplish, the Actions you took, and the Results you
achieved.
• This method helps provide clear and concise answers
that highlight your skills and experiences.

Situation:
I led a team in developing a marketing campaign for a local
nonprofit in a college group project.
Task:
We had a tight deadline, and not all team members were
equally committed, so keeping everyone on track was
challenging.
Action:
I organized regular meetings, delegated tasks based on
strengths, and encouraged open communication to resolve
issues.
Result:
We delivered the project on time, exceeded client
expectations, and my leadership was acknowledged by my
peers and professors.

VMock Interview In-Class Activity
Objective: Provide you with a realistic interview practice experience using the VMock platform.
Instructions:
1. Login to VMock using your Lambton College credentials at http://www.vmock.com/lambtoncollege
2. Click on the ‘Interview’ tab and then on the ‘Mock Interview’ module.
3. On the right-hand side, you will see a Mock Interview called JSS VMock Interview Assignment. Click on the assessment
and follow the prompts to get started.
4. There are a total of 4 questions in the interview. Once you complete the interview, you will receive a Performance Level
score and feedback on your performance.
5. Review the feedback and keep completing the interview to increase your score. You have a total of 3 attempts.
6. Once completing your final attempt, press the submit button.
7. Complete the reflection questions to help you make the most of your interview experience and use it to further your career
development.
