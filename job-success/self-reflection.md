Career Ready Final Reflection – Part 1
Chen Qi – Self-Reflection on Weeks 1, 2, and 4

Over the past few weeks in the Job Search and Success course, I have embarked on an insightful journey of self-discovery and career exploration that has reshaped my perspective on professional growth. As someone with a strong technical background – having developed Linux servers, Windows SDKs, and even low-level FPGA access interfaces – I have often been consumed by the challenges and rewards of coding and engineering. However, the course content has encouraged me to look beyond the technical aspects and embrace a more holistic approach to career development. In this reflection, I will share my experiences from Week 1’s Career Exploration Treasure Hunt, Week 2’s Reflection for Professional Growth, and Week 4’s Understanding Your Goals and Skills module. I will use the ‘What? So What? Now What?’ framework to structure my thoughts, analyze my personal growth, and outline my next steps for professional success.

Week 1: Career Exploration Treasure Hunt – What I Learned

During Week 1, the Career Exploration Treasure Hunt activity challenged me to take a step back and consider the broader picture of my career aspirations. Initially, I was excited yet somewhat uncertain about this exercise, as my focus had always been predominantly technical. The activity asked us to identify our inspirations, motivational quotes, dream jobs, companies we admire, and skills we wish to develop. As I reflected on these questions, I realized that my passion for technology goes far beyond writing efficient code. I discovered that I am deeply inspired by innovative leaders in the tech world who have not only built successful products but have also fostered communities and transformed industries.

What?
In this activity, I was prompted to think about who inspires me and why. I recalled influential figures such as the founders of product-based companies like Microsoft and Google, and even drew parallels with inspirational leaders in open-source communities. Their ability to blend technical expertise with visionary leadership resonated with me. I recognized that my dream is not simply to become a proficient coder but to contribute to meaningful projects that have a lasting impact on society. I also noted my affinity for creative problem-solving and my desire to work in an environment where technology is used to connect and empower people.

So What?
This exercise helped me to broaden my career perspective. I realized that my technical skills are just one facet of what I bring to the table. The treasure hunt forced me to consider the importance of communication, creativity, and leadership – attributes that are critical in product-based companies and for driving innovation. I began to see that pursuing a career in web development or technology leadership requires not only mastering programming languages but also engaging with a community, understanding user needs, and effectively conveying ideas. This understanding was pivotal, as it underscored the need to balance my technical capabilities with soft skills to fully realize my career potential.

Now What?
Following this activity, I have committed to a dual path of deepening my technical expertise and enhancing my interpersonal skills. I plan to start by engaging more with online communities and tech meetups where I can practice articulating my ideas and learn from industry leaders. Additionally, I will begin setting aside time for regular reflection, perhaps through journaling or group discussions, to keep track of my evolving career aspirations and how my experiences shape my journey. I am now more determined than ever to pursue opportunities in companies that not only push technological boundaries but also value holistic personal development.

Week 2: Reflection for Professional Growth – Delving Deeper

Week 2’s module on Reflection for Professional Growth was a turning point for me. The idea that “we do not learn from experience… we learn from reflecting on experience” (John Dewey) struck a chord. In a fast-paced industry where deadlines and technical challenges dominate, I often neglected the introspection needed to grow both personally and professionally.

What?
During this module, we explored various self-reflection techniques including journaling, feedback analysis, and even mindfulness practices. I was introduced to the ‘What? So What? Now What?’ framework – a simple yet powerful tool to analyze experiences and extract lessons from them. I began by asking myself, “What did I do well in my recent projects?” and “What could I have done better?” For instance, I recalled instances during my internship where my technical output was exemplary but my communication with team members could have been clearer. This gap in my approach became a recurring theme in my reflections.

So What?
The realization that technical brilliance alone is not sufficient for professional success was both humbling and enlightening. I understood that self-reflection is the key to unlocking hidden areas of improvement, particularly in soft skills like communication, teamwork, and leadership. Reflecting on my past experiences made me acknowledge the times when I was so focused on solving a technical problem that I overlooked the value of collaborative input and clear articulation of my ideas. This insight was crucial as it illuminated the pathway for personal growth – I need to actively work on my emotional intelligence and interpersonal communication to complement my technical skills.

Now What?
In response to these insights, I have committed to incorporating regular reflection sessions into my routine. I plan to maintain a digital journal where I document not only my technical achievements but also the challenges I face in team interactions and project management. By systematically applying the ‘What? So What? Now What?’ framework after each project or significant event, I will be able to pinpoint specific areas for improvement and track my progress over time. I am also considering seeking structured feedback from peers and mentors to gain a more rounded perspective on my performance. This approach will ensure that I am not only advancing my coding skills but also becoming a more well-rounded professional capable of leading and inspiring teams.

Week 4: Understanding Your Goals and Skills – Bridging the Gap

The Week 4 module was an exercise in self-assessment and goal setting, focusing on both hard and soft skills. For someone like me, whose foundation is deeply rooted in technical proficiency, this module was an eye-opener. It highlighted the critical importance of aligning my current skill set with my long-term career goals through a structured and strategic approach.

What?
In this module, I was tasked with a detailed skills assessment, evaluating my competencies in various areas ranging from technical skills like C++ and Linux to soft skills such as communication and teamwork. I identified that while I am proficient in many technical domains, there are significant gaps in areas such as leadership, adaptability, and even some modern programming frameworks like React.js that are increasingly demanded in the industry. The exercise involved not just listing skills but also reflecting on how these skills are perceived in the context of my desired career path.

So What?
This self-assessment was particularly enlightening because it allowed me to see the discrepancies between my current abilities and the expectations of the competitive tech landscape. For example, while I am comfortable with low-level programming and system development, the modern market values a well-rounded professional who can navigate both technical challenges and interpersonal dynamics. The realization that I need to evolve by integrating modern web development tools and improving my soft skills has become a central pillar in my career strategy. Moreover, the module emphasized the importance of setting SMART goals – Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound – to create a clear roadmap for improvement.

Now What?
Armed with the insights from the skills assessment, I have formulated a SMART goal that will guide my professional development over the coming months. Specifically, I plan to dedicate 6-8 hours per week to learning React.js – a critical tool for modern web development – and to build a portfolio of at least three projects (such as a to-do app, a weather app, and a personal portfolio website) within the next 16 months. This goal is not only specific and measurable but also directly relevant to my aspiration of working for a product-based company where modern web frameworks are essential. In addition, I will seek opportunities to improve my soft skills by engaging in group projects, participating in public speaking workshops, and actively soliciting feedback from my peers and mentors. This dual-focus strategy will enable me to become both a technically proficient and a well-rounded professional.

Integrating the ‘What? So What? Now What?’ Framework

A recurring theme throughout these weeks has been the effective use of the ‘What? So What? Now What?’ framework to facilitate deeper reflection and actionable insights. For every in-class activity and reflective exercise, I consciously applied this framework to dissect my experiences:

What?: I clearly identified the activities and experiences that stood out, such as the Treasure Hunt that highlighted my passion for technology and the introspective sessions that revealed my communication gaps.
So What?: I analyzed the implications of these experiences, understanding that my technical strengths needed to be complemented by improved interpersonal skills, and recognizing the evolving demands of the tech industry.
Now What?: I set concrete, actionable goals – like learning React.js and engaging more with industry communities – to address these identified gaps and to continue my personal and professional development.
This structured approach not only made my reflections more coherent but also provided a clear pathway for continuous improvement. It has reinforced my belief that reflection is not merely a passive exercise, but a critical tool for planning and executing a successful career trajectory.

Before this course, my focus was largely technical. I viewed every challenge as a coding problem to be solved with logic and persistence. However, through the activities and reflections over these weeks, I have come to appreciate the broader spectrum of skills necessary for career success. The activities helped me realize that effective communication, leadership, and emotional intelligence are just as important as technical acumen. This shift in perspective is transformative because it means that my growth as a professional is no longer confined to the realms of programming and development. Instead, it now encompasses a holistic approach that includes strategic planning, self-awareness, and continuous learning.

The Career Exploration Treasure Hunt opened my eyes to the kind of impact I want to have – not just in writing code, but in creating solutions that truly resonate with people. The Reflection for Professional Growth module made me understand the value of looking back at my experiences to learn and grow, while the Skills Assessment and Goal Setting worksheet provided a concrete plan to bridge the gap between where I am and where I aspire to be.

As I move forward, I will carry these lessons with me. I am now more committed to pursuing a career that blends technical innovation with personal development. I plan to actively seek roles that challenge me to think creatively, work collaboratively, and continuously learn new technologies. Whether it is through pursuing further studies, engaging in industry conferences, or even taking on leadership roles in projects, I am excited about the prospect of evolving not only as a software engineer but also as a well-rounded professional.
