C O N N E C T I O N S
• Connect with professionals in your field
• Aim for quality over quantity
• Look for commonality between you and
the connection (did you graduate from
the same school, take the same
program, have a connection in common,
are they a co-op student?)
• Personalize connection requests
REMEMBER! The main focus of connecting
with people on LinkedIn should be to build
and nurture professional relationships that
can lead to mutual benefits.

I N F O R M A T I O N A L
I N T E R V I E W
An informational interview is a meeting
where you seek advice and insights
from someone working in a field or
company of interest.
• Gain insights into a career path.
• Build your professional network.
• Learn about industry trends and company
culture.
• Receive advice on how to enter and succeed in
the field.
• Focus is on learning and building connections
(not about asking for a job)
• Learn about the industry and the company of the
interviewee.
• Understand current trends and challenges.
Research
•
Determine what you want to learn from the interview.
Set Objectives
• Can you describe your career path and how you got to your
current position?
• What do you enjoy most about your job?
• What are the biggest challenges you face in your role?
• What skills are most important for success in your industry?
• Can you suggest any resources for someone looking to
enter this field?
Prepare Questions
P R E P A R I N G F O R
A N
I N F O R M A T I O N A L
I N T E R V I E W
• Greet and thank the interviewee. Briefly introduce
yourself.
• Ask your prepared questions, listen actively, and
take notes.
• Ask follow-up questions based on their responses.
• Thank the interviewee for their time and insights.
S T R U C T U R E :
• Encourage detailed responses.
• Show interest, nod, and provide feedback.
• Dig deeper into interesting points.

I N - C L A S S A C T I V I T Y
I N F O R M A T I O N A L I N T E R V I E W W I T H A N A I
C H A T B O T
Objective: Practice conducting an informational interview and learn about a specific job role or industry
you're interested in by interacting with an AI chatbot.
Instructions:
1.
Begin by defining a clear objective for your informational interview. Decide what specific insights
you want to gain (e.g., potential career growth opportunities, essential skills for the role, company
work-life balance, etc.).
2. Based on this objective, formulate a list of five questions you would like to ask during your
informational interview. (For inspiration, refer to the list of sample questions available on D2L.)
3. After you have prepared your list, input the following prompt into ChatGPT:
“You are an HR professional with 20 years experience interviewing candidates and selecting the most
suitable ones. You understand what job roles involve and what you need to do them effectively. I will be
performing an informational interview with you I want you to educate me about becoming a {enter job
role/industry}. I will be asking you questions one by one.”
•
Once you’ve entered the prompt and pressed enter, you can start asking the questions to the chatbot.
•
Go through each question one by one. Please dig deeper into interesting points and feel free to adjust
your questions as your interview progresses.
Reflect on the interview (see reflection questions on next slide).
Reflection
What: What did you learn about the job or industry that was new or surprising to you?
So What: Why is the information you learned important for your career development?
How does it change or confirm your understanding of the job role/industry?
Now What: What steps will you take next based on the insights gained from the
informational interview? How will you apply this knowledge to your job search or
career planning?
